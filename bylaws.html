Constitution of Congo Tamil Association (CTA) — previously known as TYCA · Kinshasa, 24 April 2022

1 · Name
  • 1.1 The name shall be CONGO TAMIL ASSOCIATION (abbreviated CTA).
  • 1.2 This is neither a political party nor a religious entity. It is a secular, non-profitable and non-partisan organization.
2 · Definitions
  • Office Bearers – individually elected administrators: President, Vice President, Secretary, Joint Secretary, Treasurer, Deputy Treasurer, Cultural Secretary and Joint Cultural Secretary.
  • Working Committee – minimum of 6 members selected and appointed by the office bearers.
  • Executive Committee – the office bearers, working committee and advisors.
  • Sub Committee – nominated members; Advisors – members nominated by the office bearers.
  • Member – one who has paid the subscription; Voter – any member; Self-Nomination – all eligible members may nominate themselves.
  • Registered office shall be within Kinshasa, D.R. Congo. Tenure of office bearers and committees is 2 years. Auditors are appointed by the Executive Committee.
3 · Objectives
  • Provide an environment for social interaction and awareness of Tamil culture – art, music, drama, literature, sports and tradition – and opportunities for mental, moral and spiritual advancement.
  • Maintain a library; liaise with similar organisations worldwide; publish newsletters, brochures and periodicals.
  • Provide guidance / counselling to facilitate members' stay in the Democratic Republic of Congo.
  • Provide charity in cash or kind to the poor and needy; organise cultural events.
  • Raise funds through subscriptions, donations, game events, lucky draws and lawful commercial activities; buy, rent or build centres.
  • Establish a centralized organisation of Tamil Sangams and Tamil organisations in D.R. Congo / Republic of Congo.
4 – 5 · Membership & Subscription
  • Any person over 18 living in D.R. Congo with interest and aptitude for promoting Tamil culture is eligible. Membership covers dependent children under 18.
  • Members pay the monthly subscription prescribed by the Executive Committee, payable in advance from the month of joining.
  • Visiting family of existing members may pay one month's subscription per function per adult.
6 · Office Bearers
  • Eight office bearers are elected at the Annual General Body Meeting: President, Vice President (reserved for ladies), Secretary, Joint Secretary (reserved for ladies), Cultural Secretary, Joint Cultural Secretary (reserved for ladies), Treasurer and Deputy Treasurer.
  • Term of 2 years; eligible for re-election, but no one may hold the same position for more than two consecutive terms.
  • Anyone who ceases to be a member ceases to be an office bearer. Permanent vacancies are filled by the Executive Committee.
7 – 8 · Duties & Role of Office Bearers
  • President – administrative head; presides over meetings; safeguards the dignity of CTA; presents the Annual Budget Forecast.
  • Vice President – performs the President's duties in absence.
  • Secretary / Joint Secretary – convenes and conducts meetings, records and publishes minutes, preserves records, maintains the Register of Members.
  • Cultural Secretary – organises cultural events and operates the Association's social media.
  • Treasurer / Deputy Treasurer – receives money and issues receipts, keeps financial records, presents annual accounts to the Auditors and the AGM.
  • Office bearers run the Association's day-to-day affairs, nominate Sports, Educational (Tamil School, Hindi School) and Publications & Magazines (Website, News, Tamil Charal) committees, appoint advisors, and may call an Extra Ordinary General Body Meeting.
9 – 11 · Working, Executive Committees & Advisors
  • Working Committee members must attend meetings and carry out assigned duties; unexplained absence from 2 of the last 4 consecutive meetings ends their post.
  • The Executive Committee meets at least once a month, conducts all events, adopts financial statements, fills vacancies and interprets the constitution.
  • Advisors attend meetings, help resolve members' issues amicably and cooperate with the office bearers.
12 · Termination / Resignation
  • A member may resign by letter to the Secretary, effective on Executive Committee approval.
  • Membership may be suspended if conduct harms the reputation or dignity of the Association; the member has the right to be heard.
  • Subscriptions are not refunded, except advance subscription on written request with genuine reason. Members unpaid for over 4 months are followed up personally before membership ceases.
13 – 14 · General Body Meetings & Procedure
  • Two types: Annual General Body Meeting (before end of January, on a Sunday or public holiday, 14 days' written notice) and Extra Ordinary General Body Meeting.
  • AGM agenda: previous minutes, annual accounts, confirmation of sub-committees and advisors, appointment of auditors, other matters.
  • Quorum for a General Body Meeting is 25% of members; resolutions are decided by show of hands (ballot if the majority demands), with the Chairman holding a casting vote.
  • An Extra Ordinary meeting may be called by a two-thirds majority of the Working Committee or by written request of 25% of members.
15 – 16 · Elections & Eligibility
  • Elections are held once every two calendar years, conducted by a 3-member Election Administrator committee appointed by the Executive Committee. Secret ballot; no proxy voting.
  • Candidates must have completed two years of service in DRC and as a member, have no subscription dues, have served on a committee for 1 year, and be able to read and speak Tamil. Treasurer, Joint Treasurer and Auditor candidates need a finance / accounts background.
  • President – 2 years as a member; other positions – 1 year as a member; all need 1 year as a committee member.
17 – 18 · Auditors & Funds
  • An Auditor is appointed for the following year by the AGM; accounts are open to inspection at any time. The Auditor ensures handover of official email IDs, social media IDs and WhatsApp group admin rights to new office bearers.
  • Financial year: 1 January to 31 December. Funds are deposited in the name of CTA in a bank approved by the Executive Committee; cheques are signed by the Treasurer and one other office bearer.
  • The Annual Budget is adopted at the AGM; any change of ±10% requires a revised budget to be approved.
19 – 22 · Amendment, Dissolution, Inspection & Indemnities
  • The constitution may be amended by a Special Resolution adopted by at least a three-fourth majority of members present at a General Body Meeting.
  • CTA may be dissolved only by a General Body Meeting resolution with 14 days' notice, with written permission from the Registrar of Society. Remaining assets go to non-profit organisation(s) registered in D.R. Congo.
  • Books of account and the list of members are open to inspection by any member on 7 days' written notice.
  • No office bearer or committee member is personally liable for damage or claims against CTA, except in cases of negligence, wilful default, breach of duty or breach of trust.
